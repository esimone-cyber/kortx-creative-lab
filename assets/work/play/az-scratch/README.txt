VISIT ARIZONA — 300 x 600 INTERACTIVE SCRATCH MOCK
Open index.html in a modern browser. All display assets are local; no CDN, font, library or tracking dependencies.

Scratch with a mouse/finger and the cover clears at 32% scratched area, revealing the carousel. Focus the scratch panel and press Enter or Space for the keyboard reveal alternative. Three cards auto-advance every 4.5 seconds. Arrows, dots, horizontal swipes and keyboard arrows navigate. Hover/focus pauses automatic cycling; there is no visible pause or replay control. Reload index.html to replay. Reduced-motion preference is respected.
