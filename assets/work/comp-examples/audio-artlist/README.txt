Artlist Soundtrack Ad — 300 x 600

Unzip and open index.html in a modern browser. Original artwork and both original MP3 sound previews are included locally. No external runtime or server required.

The original entrance animation repeats every 10 seconds. Click the car or waterfall card to hear its sound preview. Choosing the other card pauses the previous sound. Returning to a sound resumes it; an ended sound restarts on click. No audio autoplays before interaction.

Pause/Resume pauses and resumes both animation and active audio. Selecting a sound while paused resumes the ad and plays that selection.

Browse Plans and the background open https://artlist.io/, matching the original destination.

The ad element emits custom adinteraction events: sound_select, pause, resume and clickout. This standalone recreation does not include Celtra reporting; connect these events in the host platform if tracking is required.
