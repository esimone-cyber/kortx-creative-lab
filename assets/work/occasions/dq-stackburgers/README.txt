DAIRY QUEEN SIGNATURE STACKBURGERS | 300 x 600
Unzip and open index.html. All visual assets and fonts are bundled.

Includes looping HTML/CSS entrance animation, Find Locations slide transition,
map/list toggle, eight selectable locations, directions links, zoom and drag
(zoom in to pan), reset and return-to-ad controls. Escape closes detail/list/map.

The map is a bundled visual recreation of the provided video, not a live maps
API. Locations and example distances match the source preview; they do not
recalculate for the viewer. Google Maps directions links require internet.
Brand artwork clicks through to https://www.dairyqueen.com/en-us/.

This standalone recreation does not include Celtra tracking. Integrators can
listen to the window 'ad-interaction' CustomEvent and connect their platform.
Edit locations and destinations in script.js. Respects reduced-motion settings.
