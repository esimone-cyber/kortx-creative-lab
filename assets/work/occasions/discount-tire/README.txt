DISCOUNT TIRE — 300 x 600 HTML AD

Upload instructions
1. Unzip this package.
2. Upload index.html, style.css, ad.js and the assets folder together to a
   directory on your website, for example /ads/discount-tire/.
3. Embed the ad with:

<iframe src="/ads/discount-tire/index.html" width="300" height="600"
  title="Discount Tire offers" style="border:0;display:block;"
  scrolling="no" allow="autoplay"></iframe>

Change the iframe path if you upload to a different directory.
No build step, backend, or external JavaScript libraries are required.
Keep the assets folder structure intact, including the MP4 animation.

Includes the approved left-edge fade, animated headline and wheel, and
three-second looping brand carousel. Brand cards open their respective
promotion pages; the main ad and Shop Deals link to discounttire.com.
The address and phone retain the reference's Auburn Hills location.
Offer wording and dates are retained from the supplied reference artwork.
