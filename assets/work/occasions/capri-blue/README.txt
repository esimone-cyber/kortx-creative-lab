CAPRI BLUE | 300 x 600 INTERACTIVE HTML AD
Unzip and open index.html. Artwork and fonts are bundled for local playback.

Includes the animated product intro and pattern background, all three category
tabs, nine product cards, animated category colors and copy, individual product
links, and Shop Now. Product names, prices and URLs match the supplied creative.

Tap a category. Swipe horizontally on a touch screen or click and drag with a
mouse. Product dots and Left/Right arrow keys also navigate cards. Each category
remembers its position. Dragging does not trigger a product clickout. Animation
respects reduced-motion settings. The intro plays once, then the catalog stays
available for interaction; reloading replays the intro.

Edit content and product URLs in data.js. Outbound links need internet access.
This is a standalone recreation, without Celtra runtime or Celtra reporting.
Optional analytics hooks: window 'ad-interaction' CustomEvent.
