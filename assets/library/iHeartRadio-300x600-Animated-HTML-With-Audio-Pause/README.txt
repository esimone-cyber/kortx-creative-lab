iHeartRadio Podcast Ad — 300 x 600

Unzip and open index.html in a modern browser. All artwork, fonts and original MP3 previews are included locally. No server or external runtime is required.

The original entrance sequence repeats every 10 seconds. Click any podcast cover to play its original preview; selecting another pauses the previous clip. Returning to a podcast resumes its position. Finished clips restart on the next click.

Pause/Resume freezes and resumes both the animation and playing audio. Selecting a podcast while paused resumes the ad and plays that selection. Audio begins only after a user click.

The background opens iheart.com. The store badges open their respective app listings. The Google Play URL was corrected from the malformed original.

Custom adinteraction events are emitted on the ad element for podcast_select, pause, resume and clickout. This standalone file does not include Celtra reporting; connect these events in the host platform if tracking is needed.
