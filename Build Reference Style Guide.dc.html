Every animation type in the system

The demos loop here so each type can be read at a glance. On the page itself every one of them is driven by scroll, drag or hover — nothing loops.

Scroll reveal
600ms · fade + 12px rise

The system's default arrival. A block fades in and rises 12px as it enters the viewport, and folds back out when it leaves, so the reveal replays on every pass. Never a scale-in, never a slide from the side.

Value change flash
760–900ms · cyan wash + glow, in place

When a feed rewrites a field, that cell lights up where it sits. The trigger is a diff of what actually rendered — not of the rules — so only genuinely changed values flash, and never the ones that merely re-evaluated.

Scrub with hold zones
play 45–62% · hold to 86% · cross 14%

Drag a handle to move through scenes. Each scene owns an equal band: its choreography plays over the front of the band, then holds fully settled, and only the tail crosses to the next — so stopping anywhere shows a finished frame, not a half-played one.

threshold ~32% cleared · hands off once

A gesture that earns its payoff. The cover clears under the pointer, and once enough of it is gone the unit hands over to what was underneath — it never re-covers, and the reveal is one-way.
